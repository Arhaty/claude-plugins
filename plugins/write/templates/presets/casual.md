# Tone of voice

Preset: casual. For community posts, chat, newsletters, changelogs and product copy with a
personal voice. Written by `/write:setup`; edit it freely. Where this file and the `write` skill
disagree, this file wins.

## Language

Write in {{language}}, the way you'd say it to a colleague. In English, numerals for every number
in copy ("3 steps"), and "5%" for percentages.

## Register

- **Contractions everywhere.**
- **"I" and "you".** Talk to one reader.
- **Short sentences, short paragraphs.** One or two sentences a paragraph is fine.
- **A sentence fragment is allowed** when it answers something ("Yes." "Two days."), never as a
  dramatic opener.
- **Humor** when it is true to the subject, never as a setup for the point.

## Brevity

Casual still means short. Say the thing, give the detail that matters, stop.

- No warm-up ("So, I've been thinking..."), no sign-off speech.
- No filler intensifiers: "really", "super", "literally", "actually".

## What the writing avoids

- Em dashes. Commas, periods and parentheses do the job.
- Hype: "game-changing", "insane", "next level", "you won't believe".
- Engagement bait: "Thoughts?", "Smash that like".
- More than one exclamation mark in a piece. Emojis only in chat, at most one, never as a bullet.
- Fake intimacy: "Let's be real", "Between you and me", "Real talk".
- In-jokes and slang a new reader wouldn't get.

## By medium

- **Chat and comments.** No structure. Answer first.
- **Newsletters.** One topic per issue or one per section. Link the source.
- **Changelogs.** What changed for the user, in their words, one line each.
- **Product copy.** What it does, in the fewest words. Buttons are verbs.
